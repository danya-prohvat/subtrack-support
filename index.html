❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is SubTrack free?

Yes! SubTrack is completely free, with no ads.

How do I add a subscription?

Open the app, tap the "Add" button, choose a service from the list or create your own, enter the price, billing cycle and next payment date, and save.

Can I edit or delete a subscription?

Yes! Open the subscription details, make changes, and save. To delete, use the delete option in the subscription details.

How do I mark a subscription as cancelled?

Open the subscription details and mark it as cancelled. It will move to the "Cancelled" tab, and SubTrack will show how much you've saved.

How do I cancel a subscription with the provider?

Open the subscription details and use the manage / cancel link to go to the provider's page. Subscriptions bought through the App Store can be cancelled in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Can I use different currencies?

You can choose the app's currency from 87 currencies in Settings. All subscriptions are shown in the selected currency.

Notifications are not working

Try these steps:

  • iPhone Settings → Notifications → enable notifications for SubTrack
  • Check Focus / Do Not Disturb settings (they can block alerts)
  • Make sure the subscription has the correct next payment date
  • Force close the app and reopen it
  • Restart your device
  • Make sure you have the latest version from the App Store
  • Check if your iOS version is up to date

Will I lose my subscriptions if I reinstall the app?

If iCloud sync is turned on, your subscriptions will be restored from your iCloud account. If iCloud sync is turned off, data stored only on your device will be removed when you delete the app.

How can I export my data?

Go to Settings and use the export option to save your subscriptions as a PDF or CSV file.
