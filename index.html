❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is SubTrack free?

Yes. SubTrack is completely free, with no ads.

How do I add a subscription?

Open the app, tap Add, choose a service from the list or create your own, enter the price, billing cycle and next payment date, then save.

How do I edit or delete a subscription?

Open the subscription details to edit it and save your changes. To delete a subscription, use the delete option in the subscription details.

How do I mark a subscription as cancelled?

Open the subscription details and mark it as cancelled. It will move to the Cancelled tab, and SubTrack will show how much you have saved.

How do I cancel a subscription with the provider?

Open the subscription details and use the manage / cancel link to go to the provider's page. Subscriptions bought through the App Store can be cancelled in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Can I use different currencies?

You can choose the app's currency from 87 currencies in Settings. All subscriptions are shown in the selected currency.

Notifications not working?

  • Make sure notifications are enabled in iPhone Settings → Notifications → SubTrack
  • Check that Focus or Do Not Disturb mode is not blocking notifications
  • Check that the subscription has the correct next payment date
  • Force close the app and reopen it
  • Restart your device
  • Update the app to the latest version from the App Store
  • Check for iOS updates

What happens if I delete and reinstall the app?

If iCloud sync is turned on, your subscriptions will be restored from your iCloud account. If iCloud sync is turned off, data stored only on your device will be lost when you delete the app.

How can I export my data?

Go to Settings and use the export option to save your subscriptions as a PDF or CSV file.
